# UIUC LAS BSLAS Common Graduation Requirements

- **Catalog year:** 2026–2027
- **Institution:** University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign
- **College and degree:** College of Liberal Arts & Sciences, BSLAS
- **Official source:** [Degree and General Education Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/general-information/degree-general-education-requirements/)

This document contains the university and LAS requirements shared by the Mathematics and Statistics BSLAS programs. Program-specific requirements and notes about major courses that fulfill General Education categories are kept in the corresponding program document.

## Minimum Graduation Hours

A minimum of **120 total credit hours** is required for graduation.

## University Requirements

- Complete at least **40 hours of upper-division coursework**, generally at the 300 or 400 level. These hours may come from any part of the degree.
- University and residency requirements are governed by [Student Code § 3-801](https://studentcode.illinois.edu/article3/part8/3-801/) and the Academic Catalog.

## Language Other Than English

LAS students in the Sciences and Letters curriculum are expected to reach proficiency equivalent to completion of the fourth college semester in a language other than English. Depending on prior study and placement, the requirement accounts for **0–20 credit hours**.

The requirement may be met in any of these ways:

- Complete a fourth-semester college-level language course.
- Complete four years of the language in high school.
- Complete the third-semester level in each of two different languages through an accepted combination of high-school and college study.
- Demonstrate fourth-semester proficiency through an examination approved by LAS and the appropriate department.

Students should use placement and proficiency guidance from LAS and their academic advisor when determining whether additional language coursework is needed.
