## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Choose a second major, a minor, or at least 12 hours of thematically related coursework outside Political Science, developed with an academic advisor. The requirements table assigns this group 12 hours.

## Major Electives

| Group | Selection rule | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| Lower-level electives | Additional 100- or 200-level Political Science courses | 9 |
| Advanced electives | 300- or 400-level Political Science courses | 15 |
| Other major electives | Political Science courses at any level | 6 |

At most 6 hours of PS 494 — Junior Honors Seminar may count toward the advanced requirement. At most 6 hours each of PS 490 — Individual Study, PS 491 — Internship, and PS 492 — UG Research Assistance may count toward the major; their combined maximum is 9 hours. PS 496 — Senior Honors Thesis cannot count toward the 45-hour major minimum. Titles are supplied by the [official PS course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/ps/).

The catalog's introductory text states that major and supporting coursework normally totals 70 hours, but the General Political Science requirement table totals 45 hours; the course groups above retain the table's requirements.
