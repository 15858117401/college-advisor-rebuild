# Biology — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Undergraduate program index — Biology](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/)

The official program index does not list a standalone Biology degree. Its Biology entry directs readers to [Integrative Biology, BSLAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/integrative-biology-bslas/#degreerequirementstext) or [Molecular & Cellular Biology, BSLAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/molecular-cellular-biology-bslas/#degreerequirementstext). These are separate majors; neither is substituted for a standalone Biology program here.
