# Philosophy, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Philosophy, BALAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/philosophy-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Required course or choice | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| PHIL 202 — Symbolic Logic | 3 |
| PHIL 203 — Ancient Philosophy | 4 |
| PHIL 206 — Early Modern Philosophy | 4 |
| PHIL 421 — Ethical Theories | 3 |
| PHIL 426 — Metaphysics **or** PHIL 430 — Theory of Knowledge | 3 |
| PHIL 499 — Capstone Seminar | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete at least **12 hours** using one of the following alternatives. Develop the specific coursework with a departmental advisor's help and approval.

- **Option I — Intensive study in another discipline:** Courses in one other discipline, normally above the 100-level. Most approved minors satisfy this requirement; a second major may also satisfy it.
- **Option II — Thematic study:** Courses outside philosophy in one or more disciplines, normally above the 100-level, organized around one theme or topic.

## Major Electives

Complete at least **12 additional hours in Philosophy**, including **9 hours above the 100-level** and at least **two 300- or 400-level courses**. Required courses and electives together must include at least **32 hours of Philosophy**.
