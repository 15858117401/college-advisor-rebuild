# Statistics, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Statistics, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/statistics-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

The catalog’s introductory major/supporting-coursework total is 70–72 hours, but its detailed requirement table totals 42–44 hours and identifies no additional coursework accounting for the difference. The listed requirements are retained below.

### Statistical core — 13 hours

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| STAT 400 — Statistics and Probability I | 4 |
| STAT 410 — Statistics and Probability II | 3 |
| STAT 425 — Statistical Modeling I | 3 |
| STAT 426 — Statistical Modeling II | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Introductory and preparatory coursework — 17–19 hours

Calculus through MATH 241 — Calculus III: **11–12 hours**.

Choose one introductory statistics course for **3–4 hours**:

- STAT 107 — Data Science Discovery
- STAT 200 — Statistical Analysis
- STAT 212 — Biostatistics

Choose one linear algebra course for **3 hours**:

- MATH 257 — Linear Algebra with Computational Applications
- MATH 415 — Applied Linear Algebra
- MATH 416 — Abstract Linear Algebra

## Major Electives

- STAT 430 — Topics in Applied Statistics

- STAT 432 — Basics of Statistical Learning
- STAT 433 — Stochastic Processes
- STAT 434 — Survival Analysis
- STAT 440 — Statistical Data Management
- STAT 443 — Professional Statistics
- STAT 447 — Data Science Programming Methods
- STAT 448 — Advanced Data Analysis
- STAT 480 — Big Data Analytics
