### Computer systems alternatives — 8–11 hours

Complete **one entire alternative**:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.
- **Alternative B:** CS 340 — Introduction to Computer Systems **and two** 400-level CS courses numbered above CS 403, excluding CS 421 and CS 491.

### Probability and statistics choice — 3 hours

Choose one:

- STAT 200 — Statistical Analysis
- STAT 212 — Biostatistics
- CS 361 — Probability & Statistics for Computer Science

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Mathematics

Complete each row.

| Course or choice | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I | 5 (catalog choice row) |
| MATH 225 — Introductory Matrix Theory **or** MATH 257 — Linear Algebra with Computational Applications | 2 or 3 |
| MATH 231 — Calculus II | 3 |

The catalog prints 5 hours beside the MATH 220 / MATH 221 choice. The [official MATH entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/math/) specify 5 hours for MATH 220 and 4 for MATH 221.
