## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Component 1: regional language — 6 advanced hours

Complete three years of college-level Russian or another East European or Eurasian language, or demonstrate equivalent proficiency. Only the third-year language coursework, beyond the first two prerequisite years, counts toward this 6-hour component. Prior regional-language coursework used for the LAS language requirement may satisfy the first two years; otherwise the full regional-language sequence is required in addition to that coursework.

### Component 3: single discipline — 18 hours

Complete courses in one discipline in consultation with the advisor. Common disciplines are anthropology, economics, geography, history, political science, Russian language and literature, and sociology. The catalog also permits business administration, comparative literature, education, English, fine arts, French, German, journalism, linguistics, mathematics, music, philosophy, psychology, natural sciences, and other advisor-approved disciplines.

If language study is used for this component, complete 20 hours beyond the additional 6 language hours specified in Component 1, rather than the table's usual 18-hour component.

## Major Electives

### Component 2: additional regional studies courses — 15 hours

Choose from the Center's applicable course list. Include one course from each of three departments other than the department used for Component 3. The remaining Component 2 courses may be in the same discipline as Component 3, but a course may count toward only one component. Basic language-skills courses focused on speaking, listening, reading, and writing cannot count toward Component 2. The catalog refers to a list maintained by the Center and does not enumerate eligible courses in this requirement.
