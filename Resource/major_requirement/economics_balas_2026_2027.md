# Economics, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Economics, BALAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/economics-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Economics core

Complete all seven courses. The catalog specifies **at least 30 hours of Economics**, including this core and the 11-hour elective group below. ECON 199, ECON 220, ECON 398, and ECON 399 do not count toward that minimum.

- ECON 102 — Microeconomic Principles
- ECON 103 — Macroeconomic Principles
- ECON 198 — Economics at Illinois
- ECON 202 — Economic Statistics I
- ECON 203 — Economic Statistics II
- ECON 302 — Inter Microeconomic Theory
- ECON 303 — Inter Macroeconomic Theory

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Mathematics — 7–8 hours

Complete **MATH 220 — Calculus or MATH 221 — Calculus I**, **and MATH 231 — Calculus II**.

### Supporting coursework — 18 hours

Complete **18 hours outside Economics related to the student's major interest in Economics**. The catalog refers students to the department for details; its [supporting-coursework requirements](https://economics.illinois.edu/academics/undergraduate-program/majors-minor/economics/undergraduate-supporting-coursework) specify:

- At least **12 hours at the 200-level or above**; the plan requires approval from an Economics advisor.
- Courses used for Economics major requirements cannot be reused, including required MATH/STAT courses. ECON courses and courses cross-listed with ECON are excluded.
- Courses must be taken for a letter grade. Credit/no-credit, proficiency, AP, IB, and A-level credit cannot count. Articulated transfer courses may count, including courses recorded as 1--, 2--, 3--, or 4--.
- Composition I, seminars, and independent studies cannot count. Language courses must be at the **fifth level or higher**.
- Double-major, dual-degree, or minor coursework may count if an advisor confirms that it meets these requirements.

## Major Electives

Complete **11 additional hours of 300- or 400-level Economics**. ECON 398 and ECON 399 are excluded; the core and elective minimum also excludes ECON 199 and ECON 220.
