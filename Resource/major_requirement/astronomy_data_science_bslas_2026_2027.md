# Astronomy + Data Science, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Astronomy + Data Science, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/astronomy-data-science-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Data science core

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| STAT/CS/IS 107 — Data Science Discovery | 4 |
| STAT 207 — Data Science Exploration | 4 |
| CS 307 — Modeling and Learning in Data Science | 4 |
| CS 277 — Algorithms and Data Structures for Data Science | 4 |
| IS 467 — Ethics and Policy for Data Science | 3 |
| IS 477 — Data Management, Curation & Reproducibility | 3 |

### Astronomy core

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| ASTR 210 — Introduction to Astrophysics | 3 |
| ASTR 310 — Computing in Astronomy | 3 |

### Research or discovery experience — 3 hours

Complete **ASTR 390 — Individual Study or ASTR 490 — Senior Thesis**. Develop the experience with an advisor before the end of the sophomore year. It must produce one or more artifacts documenting the experience, with at least 3 credit hours specifically designated for its preparation and completion.

For linear algebra, select one, **2–4 hours**; MATH 227 or MATH 257 is recommended:

- MATH 227 — Linear Algebra for Data Science.
- MATH 257 — Linear Algebra with Computational Applications.
- MATH 225 — Introductory Matrix Theory.
- MATH 415 — Applied Linear Algebra.

## Major Electives

### Advanced astronomy — 6–7 hours

Select two courses:

- ASTR 404 — Stellar Astrophysics.
- ASTR 405 — Planetary Systems.
- ASTR 406 — Galaxies and the Universe.
- ASTR 414 — Astronomical Techniques.
