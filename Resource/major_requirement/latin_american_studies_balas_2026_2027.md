# Latin American Studies, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Latin American Studies — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/latin-american-studies-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

Course selections require approval by the Associate Director of the Center for Latin American and Caribbean Studies.

| Requirement | Course or alternative | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| Introduction | LAST 170 — Que Pasa in Latin America? Cultures, Histories and Politics South of the Rio Grande | 3 |
| Integrative experience | LAST 490 — Individual Study, or an approved study abroad course, or an approved service-learning course | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete 3 hours of advanced coursework in a Latin American language beyond the LAS language requirement, or demonstrate equivalent proficiency by special examination. Eligible languages are Spanish, Portuguese, Quechua, or another Native American language indigenous to Mexico, Central America, or South America.
