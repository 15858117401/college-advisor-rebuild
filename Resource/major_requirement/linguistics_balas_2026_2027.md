# Linguistics, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Linguistics, BALAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/linguistics-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Core — 18 hours

Complete all six courses. The catalog specifies credits for the group.

- LING 100 — Intro to Language Science
- LING 210 — Language History
- LING 301 — Elements of Syntax
- LING 302 — Elements of Phonology
- LING 307 — Elmnts Semantics & Pragmatics
- LING 401 — Intro to General Phonetics

Choose one additional course for **3 hours**:

- LING 225 — Language, Mind, and Brain
- LING 250 — American Voices: Linguistic Diversity in the US

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Additional language study — 8–10 hours

Beyond the college language requirement, complete at least **8 hours in a second foreign language**. One of the two languages must be non-Western, selected from the following list or approved with the advisor: American Sign Language, Arabic, Bamana, Basque, Chinese, Hebrew, Hindi/Urdu, Japanese, Korean, Lingala, Persian, Quechua, Sanskrit, Swahili, Turkish, Wolof, Zulu.

These language courses may also count toward the linguistically relevant coursework below.

### Linguistically relevant coursework — 14 hours

Choose courses with the advisor, using one of the following alternatives:

- **Option A:** Courses in any foreign language, anthropology, classics, computer science, English, English as an international language, philosophy, psychology, speech and hearing science, or communication.
- **Option B:** For specialization in the linguistics of a particular language, complete at least four semesters of that language beyond the elementary level **and** a linguistics course or independent study focused on the language or its language family.

## Major Electives

Complete **15 hours of 200- through 400-level Linguistics courses**, selected in consultation with the advisor.
