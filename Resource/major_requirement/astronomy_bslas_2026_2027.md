# Astronomy, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Astronomy, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/astronomy-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| ASTR 210 — Introduction to Astrophysics | 3 |
| ASTR 310 — Computing in Astronomy | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Physics — 12 hours

Complete all:

- PHYS 211 — University Physics: Mechanics.
- PHYS 212 — University Physics: Elec & Mag.
- PHYS 213 — Univ Physics: Thermal Physics.
- PHYS 214 — Univ Physics: Quantum Physics.

### Mathematics — 11 hours as listed

Complete all:

- MATH 221 — Calculus I; MATH 220 — Calculus may be substituted.
- MATH 231 — Calculus II.
- MATH 241 — Calculus III.

The requirement table gives 11 hours for this group while permitting MATH 220 in place of MATH 221. The [official MATH entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/math/) give MATH 220 as 5 hours and MATH 221 as 4 hours, so the permitted MATH 220 sequence totals 12 hours.

## Major Electives

### Advanced astronomy — 9–10 hours

Select three:

- ASTR 404 — Stellar Astrophysics.
- ASTR 405 — Planetary Systems.
- ASTR 406 — Galaxies and the Universe.
- ASTR 414 — Astronomical Techniques.

### Additional technical courses — at least 9 hours

Complete at least 9 additional hours of 300- or 400-level ASTR or PHYS courses. Other 300- or 400-level technical classes, such as chemistry, computer science, engineering, or statistics, may substitute with academic adviser approval. At most 4 hours of ASTR 390 — Individual Study, or an equivalent independent study course such as PHYS 497 — Individual Study, may count toward this group. Titles are supplied by the official [ASTR 390](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/astr/#ASTR390) and [PHYS 497](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/phys/#PHYS497) entries.
