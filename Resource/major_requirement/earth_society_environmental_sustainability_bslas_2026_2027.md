## Required Major Courses

### Common core — 10 hours

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| ESE 200 — Earth Systems | 3 |
| ENSU 300 — Environmental Sustainability | 3 |
| ESE 379 — Introduction to Geographic Information Systems | 4 |

A concentration is required. Complete one of the two alternatives below in addition to the common core and advanced electives. Substitutions may be made with advisor approval.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

The major page requires and links the following concentrations. The linked 2026–2027 pages title their parent major **Environmental Sustainability**, whereas the main page uses **Earth, Society, & Environmental Sustainability**; the catalog's explicitly linked alternatives are retained here.

### Alternative 1: Science of the Earth System — 19–22 hours

Complete each requirement in this [official concentration](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/environmental-sustainability-bslas/science-of-the-earth-system/#degreerequirementstext):

| Course or choice | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I **or** CHEM 202 — Accelerated Chemistry I | 3 |
| CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I **or** CHEM 203 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab I | 1 or 2 |
| MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I | 5 or 4 |
| STAT 100 — Statistics | 3 |
| PHYS 101 — College Physics: Mech & Heat **or** PHYS 211 — University Physics: Mechanics | 5 or 4 |
| GEOL 107 — Physical Geology **or** IB 150 — Organismal & Evolutionary Biol | 4 |

The concentration table prints 1 hour beside the chemistry laboratory choice, 5 beside the calculus choice, and 5 beside the physics choice without separate hours for the alternatives. The distinct alternative hours above come from the official [CHEM](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/chem/), [MATH](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/math/), and [PHYS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/phys/) entries; its stated 19–22-hour group range is retained.
