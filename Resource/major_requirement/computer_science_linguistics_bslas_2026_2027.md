### Computer systems alternatives — 8–11 hours

Complete **one entire alternative**:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.
- **Alternative B:** CS 340 — Introduction to Computer Systems **and two** 400-level CS courses numbered above CS 403, excluding CS 421 and CS 491.

The two additional CS courses in Alternative B must be distinct from all other courses used for program requirements or options. Choose these two courses in consultation with an academic advisor.

### Probability and statistics choice — 3–4 hours

Choose one:

- STAT 200 — Statistical Analysis
- STAT 212 — Biostatistics
- CS 361 — Probability & Statistics for Computer Science

## Major Electives

### General linguistics — 6 hours

Choose **two**:

- LING 301 — Elements of Syntax
- LING 302 — Elements of Phonology
- LING 304 — Elements of Morphology
- LING 307 — Elmnts Semantics & Pragmatics
- LING 401 — Intro to General Phonetics

### Advanced linguistics — 9 hours

Choose **three** options:

- LING 442 — Computational Sociolinguistics
- LING 444 — Computational Syntax
- LING 446 — Fundamentals for Speech Signal Processing and Analysis
- LING 448 — Introductory Machine Learning **or** CS 446 — Machine Learning
- LING 490 — Special Topics in Linguistics: consult the advisor about eligible topics; may be repeated for this requirement when topics differ.
