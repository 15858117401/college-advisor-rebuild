# Psychology, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Psychology — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/psychology-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

Choose one concentration. Complete the common courses plus the required courses and elective groups for that same concentration; the ten concentrations below are alternatives. Hours in group headings are the catalog's group totals, not the credits of every listed option.

| PSYC 235 — Intro to Statistics, or equivalent | 3 |
