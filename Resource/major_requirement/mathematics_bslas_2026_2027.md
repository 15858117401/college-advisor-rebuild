### Supporting coursework outside Mathematics — 12 hours

Complete one of these approved paths:

- At least 12 advisor-approved hours in one mathematics-related area outside MATH/ASRM, including at least one advanced course. Counted courses must not also fulfill a major requirement.
- Any minor fulfilled with at least 12 hours, including one advanced course. Counted courses must not be used for the major or cross-listed with MATH/ASRM.
- Any double major or dual degree.

## Major Electives

### Breadth — 6 hours

Choose two courses:

- MATH 402 — Non Euclidean Geometry
- MATH 403 — Euclidean Geometry
- MATH 423 — Differential Geometry
- MATH 441 — Differential Equations
- MATH 446 — Applied Complex Variables
- MATH 448 — Complex Variables
- MATH 453 — Number Theory
- MATH 481 — Vector and Tensor Analysis

### Additional advanced mathematics — 6 hours

Complete two additional 400-level or approved 500-level MATH courses. Courses graded S/U do not count.
