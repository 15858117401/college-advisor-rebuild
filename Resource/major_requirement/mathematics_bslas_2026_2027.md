### Approved Supporting Coursework — 12 hours

Complete one of the following approved paths without reusing the coursework for another Mathematics major requirement:

- At least 12 advisor-approved hours in one mathematics-related area outside MATH/ASRM, including at least one advanced course.
- A minor containing at least 12 hours, including at least one advanced course; the counted courses may not be reused for the major or cross-listed with MATH/ASRM.
- A double major or dual degree.

## Mathematics Major Coursework

An optional concentration may be elected in consultation with an advisor. Students who do not elect a concentration complete the coursework in this section.

### Analysis Requirement — 3 hours

Complete one:

- MATH 444 — Elementary Real Analysis
- MATH 447 — Real Variables
- MATH 424 — Honors Real Analysis

### Breadth Requirement — 6 hours

Select two courses from this list:

- MATH 402 — Non Euclidean Geometry
- MATH 403 — Euclidean Geometry
- MATH 423 — Differential Geometry
- MATH 441 — Differential Equations
- MATH 446 — Applied Complex Variables
- MATH 448 — Complex Variables
- MATH 453 — Number Theory
- MATH 481 — Vector and Tensor Analysis

### Additional Advanced Mathematics — 6 hours

Complete two additional 400-level or approved 500-level MATH courses. Courses graded S/U may not be used for this requirement.
