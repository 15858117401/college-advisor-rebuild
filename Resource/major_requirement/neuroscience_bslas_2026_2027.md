# Neuroscience, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Neuroscience, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/neuroscience-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Introductory neuroscience — 11 hours

Complete all:

- MCB 150 — Molecular & Cellular Basis of Life.
- MCB 170 — Society and the Brain.
- PSYC 100 — Intro Psych.

### Neuroscience core — 13 hours

Complete all five requirements:

1. MCB 250 — Molecular Genetics.
2. MCB 251 — Exp Techniqs in Molecular Biol.
3. MCB 252 — Cells, Tissues & Development.
4. MCB 253 — Exp Techniqs in Cellular Biol.
5. PSYC 210 — Behavioral Neuroscience **or** PSYC 224 — Cognitive Psych.

### Advanced neuroscience — 14 hours

Complete all:

- MCB 314 — Introduction to Neurobiology.
- MCB 354 — Biochem & Phys Basis of Life.
- MCB 460 — Neuroanatomy Laboratory.
- MCB 461 — Cell & Molecular Neuroscience.
- MCB 462 — Integrative Neuroscience.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Supporting courses — 30–31 hours

Complete all requirements:

1. MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I.
2. MATH 231 — Calculus II **or** STAT 212 — Biostatistics.
3. CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I **and** CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II **and** CHEM 105 — General Chemistry Lab II.
4. CHEM 232 — Elementary Organic Chemistry I.
5. CHEM 233 — Elementary Organic Chem Lab I.
6. PHYS 101 — College Physics: Mech & Heat **and** PHYS 102 — College Physics: E&M & Modern.

## Major Electives

### Advanced neuroscience electives — at least 15 hours

Complete five additional 300- or 400-level courses, each carrying 3 or 4 hours, totaling at least 15 hours. The catalog's footnote delegates the eligible list to the School of MCB and states that it is updated each semester. The [official Neuroscience Approved Advanced Elective Courses list](https://app.mcb.illinois.edu/courses/neurosci_advanced) supplies these courses and eligibility conditions:

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| MCB 401 — Cellular Physiology | 3 |
| MCB 402 — Sys & Integrative Physiology | 3 |
| MCB 413 — Endocrinology | 3 |
| MCB 418 — Neurobiology of the Senses | 3 |
| MCB 432 — Computing in Molecular Biology | 3 |
| MCB 466 — Neuro & Molecular Pharmacology | 3 |
| MCB 493 — Special Topics Mol Cell Biol; only approved sections below | 3 |
| IB 432 — Genes and Behavior; equivalent to ANTH 432, NEUR 432, PSYC 432 | 3 |
| NEUR 302 — Applied Neuroscience; equivalent to PSYC 302 | 3 |

For MCB 493, only sections approved as advanced neuroscience courses count. The official list explicitly identifies:

- **NA:** Neurophysiological Analysis.
- **NMI:** Neurobiology of Mental Illness.
