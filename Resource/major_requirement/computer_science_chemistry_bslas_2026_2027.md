# Computer Science + Chemistry, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Computer Science + Chemistry, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/eng_las/computer-science-chemistry-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Computer science core

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| CS 124 — Introduction to Computer Science I | 3 |
| CS 128 — Introduction to Computer Science II | 3 |
| CS 173 — Discrete Structures | 3 |
| CS 222 — Software Design Lab | 1 |
| CS 225 — Data Structures | 4 |
| CS 374 — Introduction to Algorithms & Models of Computation | 4 |
| CS 421 — Programming Languages & Compilers | 3 |

### Computer systems alternatives — 8–11 hours

Complete **one entire alternative**:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.
- **Alternative B:** CS 340 — Introduction to Computer Systems **and two** 400-level CS courses numbered above CS 403, excluding CS 421 and CS 491.

### Probability and statistics choice — 3 hours

Choose one:

- STAT 200 — Statistical Analysis
- STAT 212 — Biostatistics
- CS 361 — Probability & Statistics for Computer Science

### Chemistry foundation — 12 hours

Choose one complete general or accelerated chemistry sequence for **8 hours**:

- **General:** CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I **and** CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II **and** CHEM 105 — General Chemistry Lab II.
- **Accelerated:** CHEM 202 — Accelerated Chemistry I **and** CHEM 203 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 204 — Accelerated Chemistry II.

Also choose one organic chemistry course for **4 hours**:

- CHEM 232 — Elementary Organic Chemistry I
- CHEM 236 — Fundamental Organic Chem I

### Advanced chemistry required course — 4 hours

Choose one:

- CHEM 440 — Physical Chemistry Principles
- CHEM 442 — Physical Chemistry I

## Major Electives

### Advanced chemistry — 8 additional hours

Select **8 hours of 300- or 400-level chemistry** with an advisor. Together with the required physical chemistry course, advanced chemistry must total **12 hours**; chemistry coursework must total at least **24 hours**.

The following do not count toward advanced chemistry: CHEM 315, CHEM 397, CHEM 445, CHEM 447, CHEM 492, CHEM 494, CHEM 497, CHEM 499, or any course in another unit, including BIOC or MCB.
