# Chemistry, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Chemistry, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/chemistry-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### General BSLAS pathway

This is the general Chemistry BSLAS pathway. The catalog separately lists a [Chemistry, BS specialized curriculum](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/chemistry-bs/).

The required chemistry sequences below count within the **30 hours of chemistry and biochemistry coursework** described under Major Electives.

### General chemistry

Select one entire sequence:

- CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I **and** CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II **and** CHEM 105 — General Chemistry Lab II.
- **Or:** CHEM 202 — Accelerated Chemistry I **and** CHEM 203 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 204 — Accelerated Chemistry II **and** CHEM 205 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab II.

### Organic chemistry

Select one entire sequence:

- CHEM 232 — Elementary Organic Chemistry I **and** CHEM 233 — Elementary Organic Chem Lab I.
- **Or:** CHEM 236 — Fundamental Organic Chem I **and** CHEM 237 — Structure and Synthesis.

### Physical chemistry

Select one:

- CHEM 440 — Physical Chemistry Principles.
- CHEM 442 — Physical Chemistry I.

## Major Electives

### Chemistry and biochemistry coursework — 30 hours including required courses

Complete 30 hours in CHEM or BIOC, including the required chemistry courses above. At least 12 of the 30 hours must be at the 300 or 400 level, including at least one course outside physical chemistry. These 12 hours must include CHEM 440 or CHEM 442 and may include **MCB 354 — Biochem & Phys Basis of Life** or **MCB 450 — Introductory Biochemistry**. [Official MCB entries for these titles](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/mcb/).

At most **10 hours combined** from the following courses may count toward the 30 hours; titles are supplied by the [official CHEM course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/chem/):

- CHEM 197 — Individual Study Freshman.
- CHEM 297 — Individual Study Sophomore.
- CHEM 397 — Individual Study Junior.
- CHEM 497 — Individual Study Senior.
- CHEM 499 — Senior Thesis.

CHEM 101, CHEM 108, and CHEM 199 do not count toward the 30 hours.
