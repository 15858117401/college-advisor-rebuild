## Major Electives

### Additional coursework — at least 15 hours

Complete at least 15 additional hours offered by the Gender & Women’s Studies Department, with no more than 3 hours at the 100- or 200-level. Courses must be on the approved list maintained in the department office and with the GWS advisor.

With the GWS advisor’s consent, topics courses may count for up to 3 hours toward this group: GWS 199 — Undergraduate Open Seminar (0–5 hours), GWS 295 — Beginning Topics GWS (3 hours), GWS 395 — Intermediate Topics GWS (3 hours), and GWS 495 — Advanced Topics GWS (3 or 4 hours).

With the GWS advisor’s consent, GWS 390 — Individual Study (0–3 hours) or GWS 490 — Individual Study (2–4 hours) may count for up to 3 hours toward this group. [Official GWS course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/gws/).

### Area electives — 6 hours

Complete two courses in Gender and Women's Studies. No more than one may be at the 100- or 200-level. Select from the approved list maintained by the department or GWS advisor.

Of the 33 hours in the major, at least one course must focus on transnational or non-U.S. issues. The department or GWS advisor maintains the approved list.
