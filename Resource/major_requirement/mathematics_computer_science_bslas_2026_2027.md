### Computer systems alternatives — 8–11 hours

Complete one entire alternative:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.
- **Alternative B:** CS 340 — Introduction to Computer Systems **and two** 400-level CS courses numbered above CS 403, excluding CS 421 and CS 491. These two courses must be distinct from every other course used for program requirements or options.

## Major Electives

### Advanced Mathematics and Computer Science — 18 hours

Select at least **six advanced courses**: **one from each of Groups I–V**, plus **one additional 400-level CS or MATH course**. The additional course cannot be CS 400, CS 401, CS 402, CS 403, CS 491, MATH 492, or MATH 499.

#### Group I — Choose one

- CS 361 — Probability & Statistics for Computer Science
- MATH 461 — Probability Theory
- STAT 400/MATH 463 — Statistics and Probability I

#### Group II — Choose one

- MATH 412 — Graph Theory
- MATH 413 — Intro to Combinatorics
- MATH 417 — Intro to Abstract Algebra
- MATH 427 — Honors Abstract Algebra

#### Group III — Choose one

- MATH 441 — Differential Equations
- MATH 446 — Applied Complex Variables
- MATH 484 — Nonlinear Programming

#### Group IV — Choose one

- MATH 424 — Honors Real Analysis
- MATH 444 — Elementary Real Analysis
- MATH 447 — Real Variables

#### Group V — Choose one

- MATH 414 — Mathematical Logic
- CS/MATH 473 — Algorithms
- CS/MATH 475 — Formal Models of Computation
- CS 476 — Program Verification
- CS 477 — Formal Software Development Methods
