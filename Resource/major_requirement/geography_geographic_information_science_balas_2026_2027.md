## Major Electives

### General Geography concentration — 25–27 hours

Choose 200- through 400-level Geography & GIS courses in consultation with an academic advisor. At least 6 hours must be at the 300 or 400 level. These courses must be different from those used for the core. Complete at least 35 hours of Geography & GIS coursework across the core and concentration.
