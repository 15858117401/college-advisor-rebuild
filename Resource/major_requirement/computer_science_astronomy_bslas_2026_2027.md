Complete **one entire alternative**:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.

## Major Electives

### Advanced astronomy

Complete at least **12 advanced ASTR hours**, including ASTR 310 above and both groups below. The catalog labels advanced astronomy as **12–13 hours**.

Choose **two** courses for **6–7 hours**:

- ASTR 404 — Stellar Astrophysics
- ASTR 405 — Planetary Systems
- ASTR 406 — Galaxies and the Universe
- ASTR 414 — Astronomical Techniques

Complete **2–3 additional hours of ASTR courses at the 300-level or higher**, sufficient to meet the minimum of 12 advanced ASTR hours.
