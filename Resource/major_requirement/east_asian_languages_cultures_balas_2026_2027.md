# East Asian Languages & Cultures, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [East Asian Languages & Cultures, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/east-asian-languages-cultures-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Disciplinary and period courses — 12 hours

Complete all four:

- EALC 120 — East Asian Civilizations.
- One course on East Asia dealing substantially with the period before 1800.
- One course in East Asian history.
- One course in East Asian literature.

No course may count more than once toward these requirements, and at least two courses must be at the 200-level or above.

### Colloquium or senior project — 3 hours

EALC 398 — Colloquium in EALC, **or** an approved senior project.

For the senior project option, submit to the Director of Undergraduate Study a proposal outlining the project, the course in which it will be completed, and an agreement signed by the supervising faculty member.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### East Asian language — 10–30 hours

Complete one year of a single East Asian language beyond the intermediate level: beyond CHIN 204 or CHIN 242; JAPN 204; or KOR 204 or KOR 241.

The requirement table’s listed groups total 37–57 hours, while its introductory major/supporting-coursework range is 33–57 hours; the table’s group hours are retained here.

Students testing out of the advanced language requirement (306-level) must take two additional non-language courses from East-Asian-related offerings.

## Major Electives

### Advanced courses — 12 hours

Complete four non-language courses at the 300- or 400-level.
