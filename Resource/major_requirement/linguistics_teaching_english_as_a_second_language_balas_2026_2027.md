## Required Major Courses

### Linguistics core — 15 hours

Complete every row. The catalog specifies credits for this group.

| LING 301 — Elements of Syntax |

| LING 307 — Elmnts Semantics & Pragmatics |

### TESL core — 15 hours including the four elective choices below

EIL 411 — Intro to TESL Methodology is required **in addition to** four TESL core electives listed under Major Electives. The 15-hour total applies to this five-course group.

### Capstone

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| EIL 465 — TESL in the Community | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Beyond the college language requirement, complete at least **8 hours in a second foreign language**. One of the two languages must be non-Western.

## Major Electives

### TESL core choices

Choose **four** courses as part of the 15-hour TESL core:

- EIL 422 — Engl Grammar for ESL Teachers
- EIL 445 — Second Lang Reading & Writing
- EIL 488 — Phonology for Second Language Teachers
- EIL 456 — Lang and Social Interaction I
- EIL 460 — Principles of Language Testing
- LING 450 — Sociolinguistics I
- LING 489 — Theoretical Foundations of SLA
