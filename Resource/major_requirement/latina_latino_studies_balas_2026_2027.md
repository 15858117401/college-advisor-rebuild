# Latina/Latino Studies, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Latina/Latino Studies — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/latina-latino-studies-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Requirement | Course or alternative | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| Introduction | LLS 100 — Intro Latina/Latino Studies | 3 |
| Theory and methods | LLS 385 — Theory and Methods in LLS | 3 |
| Research and writing | LLS 490 — Research and Writing Seminar, or LLS 495 — Senior Honors Thesis | 4 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete 18 hours of supporting coursework selected with an advisor, including 6 hours at the 300 or 400 level. A campus-approved minor may satisfy this group.

## Major Electives

### Thematic area A: Literature, Media and Culture — 6 hours

Choose two courses:

- LLS 240 — Latina/o Popular Culture
- LLS 242 — Intro to Latina/o Literature
- LLS 250 — Latina/os on the Bronze Screen
- LLS 259 — Latina/o Anthropology
- LLS 357 — Literatures of the Displaced
- LLS 360 — Contemporary US Latina/o Lit
- LLS 365 — Chicana/Latina Autobiography, Memoir, Testimonio
- LLS 375 — Latina/o Media in the US
- LLS 435 — Commodifying Difference
- LLS 458 — Latina/o Performance
- LLS 472 — Border Latina, Latino Cultures

### Thematic area B: Race, Gender, and Sexuality — 6 hours

Choose two courses:

- LLS 230 — Latina/o Genders & Sexualities
- LLS 235 — Race and the Politics of Reproduction
- LLS 320 — Gender & Latina/o Migration
- LLS 355 — Race and Mixed Race
- LLS 387 — Race, Gender and the Body
- LLS 392 — Chicanas&Latinas: Self&Society
- LLS 442 — Latina Literature
- LLS 465 — Race, Sex, and Deviance
- LLS 479 — Race, Medicine, and Society

### Thematic area C: History, Politics, and Society — 6 hours

Choose two courses:

- LLS 220 — Latina/o Migration
- LLS 238 — Latina/o Social Movements
- LLS 278 — Mapping Latina/o Inequalities
- LLS 279 — Mexican-American History
- LLS 280 — Caribbean Latina/o Migration
- LLS 343 — Criminalization and Punishment
- LLS 368 — Latinas/os & Public Policy
- LLS 370 — Latina/o Ethnography
- LLS 379 — Latina/os and the City
- LLS 382 — Race and Migration in Chicago
- LLS 468 — Latinas/os & the Law
- LLS 473 — Immigration, Health & Society

### Additional LLS electives — 6 hours

Select from all LLS and cross-listed LLS classes. Courses from the thematic lists above may count here only if they were not used for a thematic-area requirement; other selections are made in consultation with an academic advisor. The major requires at least 34 hours of Latina/Latino Studies coursework.
