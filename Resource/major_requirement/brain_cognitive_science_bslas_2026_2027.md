# Brain & Cognitive Science, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Brain & Cognitive Science, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/brain-cognitive-science-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| BCOG 100 — Introduction to the Brain and Cognitive Science | 3 |
| BCOG 200 — Introduction to Programming for the Brain and Cognitive Sciences | 4 |
| BCOG 458 — Advances in Brain and Cognitive Science | 3 |

Select one:

- PSYC 220 — Images of Mind.

### Required laboratory — 4 hours

Select one:

- PSYC 311 — Behavioral Neuroscience Lab.
- PSYC 331 — Cognitive Psych Lab.
- PSYC 334 — Perception Lab.
- PSYC 363 — Developmental Child Psych Lab.
- PSYC 445 — Cognitive Neuroscience Lab.
- PSYC 489 — Neural Network Modeling Lab.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Statistics — 4 hours

PSYC 235 — Intro to Statistics, or equivalent.

### Multidisciplinary breadth — 6–7 hours

Select two of the following entries, preserving the alternatives within each entry:

- ANTH 101 — Introduction to Anthropology **or** ANTH 102 — Human Origins and Culture **or** ANTH 104 — Talking Culture **or** ANTH 143 — Biology of Human Behavior.
- INFO 102 — Little Bits to Big Ideas **or** CS 125 — Introduction to Computer Science.
- LING 100 — Intro to Language Science.
- MCB 170 — Society and the Brain.
- PHIL 100 — Intro to Philosophy-ACP **or** PHIL 101 — Introduction to Philosophy.
- PHIL 102 — Logic and Reasoning **or** PHIL 103 — Logic and Reasoning QR II.
- PSYC 100 — Intro Psych.
