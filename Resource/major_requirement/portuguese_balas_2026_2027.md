# Portuguese, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Portuguese — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/portuguese-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| PORT 402 — Advanced Grammar | 3 |
| PORT 403 — Readings in Portuguese | 3 |
| PORT 404 — Studies in Luso-Brazilian Culture | 3 |
| PORT 406 — Brazilian Film | 3 |
| PORT 410 — Topics in Brazilian Literature | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete 15–18 hours of advisor-approved supporting coursework, or a minor in a related area. A minor requires at least 18 hours. Supporting areas may include humanities (comparative literature, comparative religion, linguistics, philosophy), social sciences (anthropology, geography, history, Latin American studies, political science, sociology), education, fine and applied arts, and/or journalism. Other fields or combinations of fields require approval by the Portuguese undergraduate advisor.

## Major Electives

Complete 12 additional hours of Portuguese literature/culture courses above the 100 level. The required courses and electives together must include at least 27 hours in Portuguese above the 100 level.
