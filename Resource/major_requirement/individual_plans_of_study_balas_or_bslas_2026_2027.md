# Individual Plans of Study, BALAS or BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Individual Plans of Study — official catalog requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/individual-plans-study/)

## Required Major Courses

The catalog defines an individually designed major rather than a common list of required courses. Major and supporting coursework normally totals 51–70 hours. The student and faculty advisor develop the coursework plan, which requires approval by the LAS Courses & Curricula Committee. That committee and the college determine whether the approved plan leads to the BALAS or BSLAS. The catalog provides no universal course codes, elective list, or course-by-course credits to extract.
