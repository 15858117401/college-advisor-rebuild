# Creative Writing, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Creative Writing, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/creative-writing-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Literature for Creative Writers — 6 hours

- CW 100 — Intro to Creative Writing
- CW 200 — Reading for Writers

### Craft — 3 hours

- CW 243 — The Craft Essay: Creative Reading, Reflection, and Revision

### Creative Writing Workshops — 12 hours

Complete at least one entire 3-course, 9-hour sequence:

- Poetry: CW 106 — Poetry Workshop I **and** CW 206 — Poetry Workshop II **and** CW 406 — Poetry Workshop III.
- **OR** Fiction: CW 104 — Fiction Workshop I **and** CW 204 — Fiction Workshop II **and** CW 404 — Fiction Workshop III.

Complete the remaining 3 hours with a fourth CW workshop outside the chosen sequence. CW 404 and CW 406 may each be repeated once for credit, but repetition may not fulfill this requirement.

## Major Electives

### Writing and Literature — 3 hours

Choose one:

- 3 hours of non-workshop CW coursework: CW 460 — Intro to Literary Editing, or another approved non-workshop CW course.
- **OR** 3 hours of ENGL coursework at the 200-level or above.

Depending on the topic offered, sections of CW 199 — Undergraduate Open Seminar (1–5 hours), CW 202 — Topics in Creative Writing (3 hours), and CW 463 — Adv Topics in Creative Writing (3 or 4 hours) may or may not count as non-workshop courses. CW 460 is 3 hours. [Official CW course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/cw/).

### Additional Literature Coursework — 12 hours

Complete both:

- 9 hours of approved ENGL literature coursework, chosen from the list maintained by the Creative Writing Program and English Advising Office.
- 3 hours of an ENGL Difference & Diaspora course, chosen from the list maintained by the English Program and English Advising Office.

At most 3 hours of 100-level coursework may count toward these 12 hours.
