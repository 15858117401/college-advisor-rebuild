# Geology, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Geology, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/geology-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### General BSLAS pathway — Sciences & Letters

This is the Geology BSLAS Sciences & Letters pathway. The catalog separately lists a [Geology, BS specialized curriculum](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/geology-bs/).

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| GEOL 107 — Physical Geology | 4 |
| GEOL 208 — History of the Earth System | 4 |
| GEOL 411 — Structural Geol and Tectonics | 4 |
| GEOL 417 — Geol Field Methods, Western US | 6 |
| GEOL 432 — Mineralogy and Mineral Optics | 4 |
| GEOL 436 — Petrology and Petrography | 4 |
| GEOL 440 — Sedimentology and Stratigraphy | 4 |

GEOL 100 — Planet Earth is accepted in place of GEOL 107. Students entering the curriculum after taking GEOL 100 should enroll in GEOL 208. The [official GEOL 100 entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/geol/#GEOL100) supplies its title and lists 3 hours.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

| Course or choice | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I | 3 |
| CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I | 1 |
| CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II | 3 |
| CHEM 105 — General Chemistry Lab II | 1 |
| PHYS 101 — College Physics: Mech & Heat **or** PHYS 211 — University Physics: Mechanics | 4–5 |

Select one mathematics course, **4–5 hours**:

- MATH 220 — Calculus.
- MATH 221 — Calculus I.
- MATH 234 — Calculus for Business I.

## Major Electives

Complete **3–4 hours** of advanced Geology or cognate science electives.
