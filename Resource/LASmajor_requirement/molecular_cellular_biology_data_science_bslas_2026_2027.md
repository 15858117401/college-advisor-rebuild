# Molecular and Cellular Biology + Data Science, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Molecular and Cellular Biology + Data Science, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/molecular-cellular-biology-data-science-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### MCB core — 17 hours

Complete all:

- MCB 150 — Molecular & Cellular Basis of Life.
- MCB 250 — Molecular Genetics.
- MCB 251 — Exp Techniqs in Molecular Biol.
- MCB 252 — Cells, Tissues & Development.
- MCB 253 — Exp Techniqs in Cellular Biol.
- MCB 354 — Biochem & Phys Basis of Life.

### Data science core — 29–30 hours, including mathematical foundations below

Complete all:

- STAT 107 — Data Science Discovery.
- STAT 207 — Data Science Exploration.
- CS 307 — Modeling and Learning in Data Science.
- CS 277 — Algorithms and Data Structures for Data Science.
- IS 467 — Ethics and Policy for Data Science.
- IS 477 — Data Management, Curation & Reproducibility.

### Research or discovery experience — at least 6 hours

Complete at least 6 hours through one or more of these options:

1. **MCB 290 — Undergraduate Research**, completed in School of MCB research laboratories. MCB data science-focused laboratories are encouraged. The catalog permits an optional **MCB 492 — Senior Thesis** in the MCB 290 research laboratory as part of this option.
2. **MCB 292 — Experiential Learning in MCB**.
3. A School of MCB-developed residential study abroad program focused on advanced MCB and Data Science courses, with at least the equivalent of **6 UIUC credit hours** transferred to Illinois. All courses must be selected under an MCB Study Abroad Academic Advisor's supervision and be pre-articulated.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Mathematical foundations — included in the 29–30-hour data science core

Complete both requirements:

- MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I.
- MATH 227 — Linear Algebra for Data Science **or** MATH 257 — Linear Algebra with Computational Applications.

### Chemistry — 10 hours

Complete all:

- CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I.
- CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II.
- CHEM 232 — Elementary Organic Chemistry I.

## Major Electives

### Advanced MCB laboratory — 2–4 hours

Complete at least one course from this list:

- MCB 301 — Experimental Microbiology.
- MCB 364 — Eukaryotic Cell Biology Laboratory.
- MCB 428 — Microbial Pathogens Laboratory.
- MCB 460 — Neuroanatomy Laboratory.
- BIOC 455 — Technqs Biochem & Biotech.

### Advanced courses, Group A — at least 9 hours

Complete at least three courses, totaling at least 9 hours, from this list:

- MCB 317 — Genetics and Genomics.
- MCB 421 — Microbial Genetics.
- MCB 432 — Computing in Molecular Biology.
- MCB 435 — Evolution of Infectious Disease.
- BIOC 446 — Physical Biochemistry.
- BIOP 401 — Introduction to Biophysics.

- MCB 316 — Genetics and Disease.
- MCB 320 — Mechanisms of Human Disease.
- MCB 400 — Cancer Cell Biology.
