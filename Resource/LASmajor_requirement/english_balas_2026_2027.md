## Major Electives

### English concentration

[English concentration](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/english-balas/english/#degreerequirementstext).

Of the required 15 hours at the 300-level or above, 6 hours must come from two different period areas.

**Historical Surveys — 6 hours**

Two courses from the following options:

| Course | Title |
| :-- | :-- |
| ENGL 209 | Early British Literature and Culture |
| ENGL 255 | Early American Literature and Culture |
| ENGL 259 | Early African American Literature and Culture |

Any ethnic literature survey with a significant pre-1900 focus

**Period Courses (American, British, Anglophone, etc.) — 15 hours**

6 hours: At least two courses that cover at least two of the following categories:

Medieval Literature & Culture (before 1550)

Shakespeare

Early Modern Literature & Culture (1550-1660, other than Shakespeare)

Eighteenth-Century Literature & Culture (1660-1800)

3 hours: At least one course in Twentieth-Century Literature & Culture

3 hours: At least one course in Contemporary Literature & Culture

**Elective Coursework — 9 hours**

At least three courses chosen from those controlled by or cross-listed with the Department of English (Business and Technical Writing, Creative Writing, and English)

One course in Difference and Diaspora. This requirement can be fulfilled by taking an appropriately focused course from among ENGL 350: Writing about Literature, Text, and Culture; the historical surveys; the period courses; or the elective coursework.

**Elective Coursework — 15 hours**

At least five courses chosen from those controlled by or cross-listed with the Department of English (Business and Technical Writing, Creative Writing, and English)

One course in Difference and Diaspora. This requirement can be fulfilled by taking an appropriately focused course from among ENGL 350: Writing about Literature, Text, and Culture; the cluster courses; or the elective coursework.
 
### Course-selection and double-counting rules

Approved historical-survey alternatives, period courses, clusters, elective coursework, and Difference & Diaspora selections are drawn from the applicable lists maintained by the Department of English.

Across all three concentrations, ENGL 350 may fulfill a period, cluster, elective, and/or Difference & Diaspora requirement only when it is not also used for the core requirement. To use it for these other requirements, take ENGL 350 a second time with a different topic.

For the English and Topics in English concentrations, at most one independent study course may count toward additional coursework: ENGL 290 — Individual Study (0–3 hours), ENGL 390 — Advanced Individual Study (3 hours), or BTW 290 — Individual Study (0–3 hours).
