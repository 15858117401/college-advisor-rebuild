# Sociology, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Sociology — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/sociology-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| SOC 100 — Introduction to Sociology | 4 |
| SOC 200 — Introduction to Sociological Theory | 3 |
| SOC 280 — Intro to Social Statistics | 4 |
| SOC 380 — Social Research Methods | 4 |

### Capstone — 3 hours

Choose one:

- SOC 400 — Internships
- SOC 450 — Senior Capstone Seminar
- SOC 495 — Senior Honors Seminar

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete 12 hours outside the Department of Sociology, selected in consultation with an academic advisor.

## Major Electives

Complete 14 hours of any Sociology (SOC) courses to reach the required 32 hours in Sociology.
