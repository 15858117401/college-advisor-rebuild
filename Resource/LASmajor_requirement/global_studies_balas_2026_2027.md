### Regional area studies — 9 hours

Select 200- through 400-level courses from at least two disciplinary departments. Coursework must complement the language study and be geographically related to the chosen language and study-abroad location.

### Required study-abroad coursework

Complete one fall or spring semester of full-time enrollment in an approved study-abroad program. Coursework must develop language and cultural knowledge or cultural and thematic-area knowledge. The catalog does not assign separate hours to this group.

## Major Electives

### Thematic area — at least 18 hours

Choose one approved thematic area and develop the course plan with a Global Studies advisor. Include courses from more than one department, no more than 3 hours at the 100 level, and at least 9 hours at the 300 or 400 level. The approved thematic alternatives are:

- Cultures in Contact
- Wealth and Poverty in a Globalized World
- Human Rights
- Governance, Conflict and Resolution
- Knowledge, Communication and Information Systems
- Environment, Sustainability, and Social Responsibility
- Global Health
