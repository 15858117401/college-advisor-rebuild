## Major Electives

### Introductory cluster courses — 9 hours

Complete one 100- or 200-level course from each Jewish Studies Minor cluster: Religion, Culture, and History. Select from the lists maintained by the Program in Jewish Culture and Society advisor.

### Advanced cluster courses — 9 hours

Complete three courses at the 300 level or above from any of the Religion, Culture, History, and Language clusters, using the lists maintained by the Program in Jewish Culture and Society advisor. The [minor's catalog page](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/minors/jewish-culture/#degreerequirementstext) likewise directs cluster selections to advisor-maintained lists. It separately lists language electives for the minor, but the major concentration page does not enumerate its advanced Language-cluster courses or explicitly adopt that minor elective list.
