# Italian, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Italian, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/italian-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

**Supporting course work or a minor chosen in consultation with an adviser, in one related area (or a combination, with no fewer than 8 hours in each). Areas may include, for example, any other language and literature, history, political science, biology (premed), international law (prelaw), economics, finance, business administration, education, architecture, fine arts, journalism. 1 — 15-18 hours**

The catalog footnote states that a minor consists of 16–21 hours.
