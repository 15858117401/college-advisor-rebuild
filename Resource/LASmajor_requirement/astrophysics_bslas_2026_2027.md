# Astrophysics, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Astrophysics, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/astrophysics-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Core requirements — 17 hours

Complete all:

- ASTR 210 — Introduction to Astrophysics.
- PHYS 211 — University Physics: Mechanics.
- PHYS 212 — University Physics: Elec & Mag.
- PHYS 213 — Univ Physics: Thermal Physics.
- PHYS 214 — Univ Physics: Quantum Physics.
- PHYS 225 — Relativity & Math Applications.

### Advanced astronomy and physics core

- ASTR 310 — Computing in Astronomy, included in the 15-hour advanced astronomy group below.
- PHYS 325 — Classical Mechanics I **and** PHYS 435 — Electromagnetic Fields I, included in the 12-hour advanced physics group below.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Mathematics — 18 hours as listed

Complete all five requirements:

1. MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I.
2. MATH 231 — Calculus II.
3. MATH 241 — Calculus III.
4. MATH 285 — Intro Differential Equations.
5. MATH 415 — Applied Linear Algebra.

The requirement table lists 18 hours for the group. The [official MATH entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/math/) give MATH 220 as 5 hours and MATH 221 as 4 hours; the permitted MATH 221 pathway totals 17 hours.

## Major Electives

### Advanced astronomy — 15 hours as listed, including ASTR 310

In addition to ASTR 310, select three of these four courses:

- ASTR 404 — Stellar Astrophysics.
- ASTR 405 — Planetary Systems.
- ASTR 406 — Galaxies and the Universe.
- ASTR 414 — Astronomical Techniques.

Also complete **at least 3 additional hours** of approved 300- or 400-level ASTR courses. ASTR 330, ASTR 350, and ASTR 390 are excluded from this additional coursework.

The requirement table lists 15 hours for this group. The [official ASTR entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/astr/) assign 4 hours to ASTR 414 and 3 each to ASTR 310, ASTR 404, ASTR 405, and ASTR 406, so choosing ASTR 414 brings the group to at least 16 hours.

### Advanced physics — 12 hours including PHYS 325 and PHYS 435

In addition to PHYS 325 and PHYS 435, complete **at least 6 additional hours** of approved 300- or 400-level PHYS courses. The catalog explicitly recommends the following courses; this is an open approved-course category:

- PHYS 326 — Classical Mechanics II.
- PHYS 401 — Classical Physics Lab.
- PHYS 402 — Light.
- PHYS 404 — Electronic Circuits.
- PHYS 427 — Thermal & Statistical Physics.
- PHYS 436 — Electromagnetic Fields II.
- PHYS 470 — Subatomic Physics.
- PHYS 486 — Quantum Physics I.

Titles absent from the requirement table are supplied by the [official PHYS course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/phys/). PHYS 398, PHYS 419, PHYS 420, PHYS 495, and PHYS 497 are excluded from this additional coursework.

### Advanced laboratory techniques — 3 hours as listed

At least one course taken for the advanced requirements must be one of the following; it counts within the advanced requirements:

- ASTR 414 — Astronomical Techniques.
- PHYS 401 — Classical Physics Lab.
- PHYS 402 — Light.
- PHYS 404 — Electronic Circuits.

The requirement table labels this group as 3 hours. The official [ASTR](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/astr/) and [PHYS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/phys/) entries assign 4 undergraduate hours to ASTR 414, PHYS 401, and PHYS 402, and 5 undergraduate hours to PHYS 404. The listed course-selection requirement is retained despite this credit discrepancy.
