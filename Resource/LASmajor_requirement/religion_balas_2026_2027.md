# Religion, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Religion — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/religion-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

Complete at least 30 hours in Religion, including at least 15 hours at the 300 or 400 level and no more than 9 hours at the 100 level.

### Foundational course — 3 hours

Choose one:

- PHIL/REL 230 — Philosophy of Religion Intro
- REL 231 — Happiness and the Good Life

### Capstone — 3 hours

Complete a 400-level REL course with an instructor-approved significant research project and a research paper of at least 20 pages. REL 493 — Honors Senior Thesis is an eligible option ([official course entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/rel/)). The capstone may also satisfy part of the distribution, primary-area, or secondary-area requirement.

## Major Electives

### Distribution — 12 hours

Choose courses from the lists maintained by the departmental advisor:

- Two courses in Judaism, Christianity, Islam, or religious practices of the ancient Near East.
- Two courses in Hinduism, Buddhism, Chinese and Japanese Religions, or indigenous American religious practices.

### Primary and secondary areas — 15 hours

Choose a primary and a secondary area from Buddhism, Christianity, Hinduism, Islam, Judaism, Philosophy of Religion, Religion in America, or an individually designed area approved by the departmental advisor.

| Group | Required coursework | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| Primary area | At least three courses | 9 |
| Secondary area | Two courses | 6 |

An individual course cannot count toward both the primary and secondary areas.
