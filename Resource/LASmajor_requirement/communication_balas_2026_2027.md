# Communication, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Communication, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/communication-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### General pathway (Option A)

At least 15 hours of the required 37 hours in Communication must be at the 300 level or above.

**Introduction to Public Speaking (choose one): — 3 hours**

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

For the CMN 112 option, complete its prerequisite CMN 111 — Oral & Written Comm I (3 hours). CMN 111 credit does not count toward the minimum 37 hours of Communication courses required for the major. [Official CMN course entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/cmn/).
