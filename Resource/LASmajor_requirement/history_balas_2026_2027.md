# History, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [History — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/history-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### General pathway

Complete both courses. They may count toward an area requirement or the 12-hour History elective group below. Titles and credits are supplied by the [official HIST course entries](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/hist/).

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| HIST 200 — Intro Hist Interpretation | 3 |
| HIST 498 — Research and Writing Seminar | 3 |

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete two introductory History courses at the 100 level, totaling 6 hours.

## Major Electives

Complete at least 36 hours of History courses in all, including at least 18 hours at the 300 or 400 level. The following groups are part of that minimum.

| Group | Selection rule | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| African, Asian, Global, Latin American, or Middle Eastern History | Courses at the 200 level or above, including at least 3 hours at the 300 level or above | 6 |
| European History | Courses at the 200 level or above, including at least 3 hours at the 300 level or above | 6 |
| U.S. History | Courses at the 200 level or above, including at least 3 hours at the 300 level or above; 3 hours must be U.S. Minority History | 6 |
| History electives | Courses at the 200 level or above; HIST 200 and HIST 498 may count here or toward an area requirement | 12 |

Within the 36 hours, complete 6 hours in pre-modern periods: one course defined as before 1600 and one defined as before 1800. HIST 100 — Global History and HIST 142 — Modern Europe and the World cannot satisfy this requirement. Area and pre-modern selections must come from the lists maintained by the Department of History; the catalog does not enumerate those lists.
