# Classics, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Classics, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/classics-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

Choose either the Classical Civilizations concentration or the Classical Languages concentration. Complete at least 12 hours at the 300- or 400-level in the chosen concentration.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### All concentration and track alternatives — 12 hours

Complete supporting coursework, a minor, or a second major in an area chosen by the student and approved by the Department of Classics Director of Undergraduate Studies. [Classical Civilizations requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/classics-balas/classical-civilizations/#degreerequirementstext); [Classical Languages requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/classics-balas/classical-languages/#degreerequirementstext).
