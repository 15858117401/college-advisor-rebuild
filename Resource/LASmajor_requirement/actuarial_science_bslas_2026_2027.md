# Actuarial Science, BSLAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Actuarial Science, BSLAS — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/actuarial-science-bslas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| ASRM 210 — Theory of Interest | 3 |
| ASRM 406 — Linear Algebra with Financial Applications | 3 |
| ASRM 441 — Statistics for Risk Modeling I | 4 |

Complete one entire sequence, **7–8 hours**; the ASRM sequence is preferred:

- ASRM 401 — Actuarial Statistics I **and** ASRM 402 — Actuarial Statistics II.
- **Or:** STAT 400 — Statistics and Probability I **and** STAT 410 — Statistics and Probability II.

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

### Calculus sequence — 11–12 hours

Complete all three steps:

1. MATH 220 — Calculus **or** MATH 221 — Calculus I.
2. MATH 231 — Calculus II.
3. MATH 241 — Calculus III, or equivalent.

### Computing — 3–4 hours

Select one:

- ASRM 195 — Foundations of Data Management.
- CS 101 — Intro Computing: Engrg & Sci.
- CS 124 — Introduction to Computer Science I.
- CS 125 — Introduction to Computer Science.

### Finance and related coursework

Required: **FIN 221 — Corporate Finance, 3 hours**.

Select three additional courses, **9 hours**, from:

- ACCY 200 — Fundamentals of Accounting.
- ECON 302 — Inter Microeconomic Theory.
- ECON 303 — Inter Macroeconomic Theory.
- FIN 230 — Introduction to Insurance.
- FIN 300 — Financial Markets.
- FIN 321 — Advanced Corporate Finance.
- FIN 431 — Property-Liability Insurance.
- FIN 432 — Managing Market Risks for Financial Institutions.
- FIN 434 — Employee Benefit Plans.

## Major Electives

### Advanced actuarial/statistics electives — at least 12 hours

Select three or more courses for a total of at least 12 hours:

- ASRM 409 — Stochastic Processes for Finance and Insurance.
- ASRM 410 — Investments and Financial Markets.
- ASRM 442 — Statistics for Risk Modeling II.
- ASRM 454 — Generalized Linear Models.
- ASRM 455 — Predictive Analytics.
- ASRM 461 — Loss Models.
- ASRM 462 — Advanced Loss Models, Credibility, and Ratemaking.
- ASRM 469 — Casualty Actuarial Mathematics.
- ASRM 471 — Life Contingencies I.
- ASRM 472 — Life Contingencies II.
- STAT 432 — Basics of Statistical Learning.

### Additional elective — 3 hours

Select one additional course from the preceding list **or** an approved section of [ASRM 499 — Topics in Actuarial Science](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/asrm/#ASRM499). ASRM 499 topic announcements and the course syllabus specify whether the topic is approved for use in the major.
