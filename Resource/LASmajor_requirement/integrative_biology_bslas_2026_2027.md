### General chemistry — 8–10 hours

Select one entire group:

- CHEM 102 — General Chemistry I **and** CHEM 103 — General Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 104 — General Chemistry II **and** CHEM 105 — General Chemistry Lab II.
- **Or:** CHEM 202 — Accelerated Chemistry I **and** CHEM 203 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab I **and** CHEM 204 — Accelerated Chemistry II **and** CHEM 205 — Accelerated Chemistry Lab II.

### Organic chemistry — 5–6 hours as listed

Select one entire group:

- CHEM 232 — Elementary Organic Chemistry I **and** CHEM 233 — Elementary Organic Chem Lab I.
- **Or:** CHEM 236 — Fundamental Organic Chem I **and** CHEM 237 — Structure and Synthesis.

### Physics — 8–10 hours

Select one entire group:

- PHYS 101 — College Physics: Mech & Heat **and** PHYS 102 — College Physics: E&M & Modern.
- **Or:** PHYS 211 — University Physics: Mechanics **and** PHYS 212 — University Physics: Elec & Mag.

## Major Electives

### Advanced course outside the area requirement — 3 hours

Complete a 300- or 400-level course from IB or any other unit on campus. The catalog labels this specific major requirement “Advanced Free Elective.”

### Integrative Biology advanced area courses — 15–20 hours

Complete at least two courses from the three areas below, with the two courses in different areas and at least one designated as a laboratory course. Complete additional eligible courses to reach at least 15 hours. Courses marked **lab** satisfy the area's laboratory designation.
