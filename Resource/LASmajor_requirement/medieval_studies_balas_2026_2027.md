# Interdisciplinary Studies: Medieval Studies, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Medieval Studies concentration — Degree Requirements](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/interdisciplinary-studies-balas/medieval-studies/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

### Medieval Studies concentration

Substitutions for named courses require concentration-advisor approval and are allowed only in exceptional cases.

| Course | Hours |
| :-- | --: |
| ENGL 202 / MDVL 201 / CWL 253 — Medieval Literature and Culture | 3 |

### Capstone — 3 hours

Complete a 400-level MDVL course with instructor approval for a capstone project; a medieval-related course not cross-listed with MDVL also requires approval from a Medieval Studies faculty advisor. The project must add to the normal coursework through an additional project or a longer, more research-intensive version of an existing project. It must involve interdisciplinary research and writing on a medieval topic, primary and secondary research, and advanced disciplinary methods and resources.

A student may petition to use MDVL 500 — Seminar in Medieval Studies. The [official course entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/mdvl/) assigns MDVL 500 4 hours, whereas the requirement table assigns the capstone group 3 hours.

## Major Electives

### Geographical distribution — 18 hours

Complete two courses (6 hours) from each of the following three regional groups. Up to 6 hours may be replaced with courses on medieval civilizations of Oceania or the Americas, including ANTH 278 — Climate Change & Civilization (3 hours; [official course entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/anth/)); at least one course must still be taken from each regional group.

### Additional Medieval Studies coursework — 21 hours

Choose medieval-related courses from participating departments with the concentration advisor. At least 12 hours must be at the 300 or 400 level. Up to 12 hours of appropriate language study may count with approval from a Medieval Studies faculty advisor.
