# Spanish, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Spanish, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/spanish-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

- SPAN 228 — Spanish Composition — 3 hours, included in the 33 hours of SPAN coursework below. [Official SPAN course entry](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/span/).

## Required Preparatory and Supporting Coursework

Complete 15–21 hours of supporting coursework, a minor, or a second major in an area chosen by the student and approved by the advisor. A minor generally consists of 16–21 hours.

## Major Electives

Complete 33 hours of SPAN courses at the 200-level or higher, including required SPAN 228:

- 4–6 courses at the 200-level.
- 4–6 courses at the 300- and/or 400-level.

With the advisor’s permission, up to two SPAN courses may be taught in a language other than Spanish. **Or**, up to two 200-level SPAN courses may be substituted using the following catalog-listed alternatives:

| Courses | Titles | Individual course hours |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| BASQ 401 and BASQ 402 | Beginners' Basque; Readings in Basque Studies | 3; 3 |
| CATL 401 and CATL 402 | Intensive Catalan Language; Studies in Catalan Literature | 3; 3 |
| PORT 401 and PORT 402 | Intensive Intermediate Portuguese; Advanced Grammar | 4; 3 |
| LAST 445 | Native Latin Amer Languages | 2–4 |

These substitutions do not count toward advanced hours and cannot substitute for SPAN 228. Course titles and credits: [BASQ](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/basq/), [CATL](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/catl/), [PORT](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/port/), [LAST](https://catalog.illinois.edu/courses-of-instruction/last/).
