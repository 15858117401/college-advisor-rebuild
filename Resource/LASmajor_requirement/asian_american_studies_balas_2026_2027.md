# Asian American Studies, BALAS — Major Requirements

- Catalog year: 2026–2027
- Official source: [Asian American Studies, BALAS](https://catalog.illinois.edu/undergraduate/las/asian-american-studies-balas/#degreerequirementstext)

## Required Major Courses

| Course | Title | Hours |
| :-- | :-- | --: |
| AAS 100 | Intro Asian American Studies | 3 |
| AAS 200 | U.S. Race and Empire | 3 |
| AAS 215 | US Citizenship Comparatively | 3 |
| AAS 300 | Theories of Race, Gender, and Sexuality | 3 |

## Major Electives

**Additional Coursework — 15 hours**

Complete at least 15 additional hours of substantive coursework offered by Department of Asian American Studies listed on the Approved Additional Coursework List.

**Electives — 6 hours**

Choose 6 hours from the Approved Elective Course List.

Course selection must be made with the advisor to form a coherent program of study. The Approved Additional Coursework List and Approved Elective List are maintained in the department office and with the AAS advisor; the catalog does not enumerate their courses.
