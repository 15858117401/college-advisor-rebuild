### Computer systems alternatives — 8–11 hours

Complete **one entire alternative**:

- **Alternative A:** CS 233 — Computer Architecture **and** CS 341 — System Programming.
- **Alternative B:** CS 340 — Introduction to Computer Systems **and two** 400-level CS courses numbered above CS 403, excluding CS 421 and CS 491.

The two additional CS courses in Alternative B must be distinct from all other courses used for program requirements or options.

### Probability and statistics choice — 3 hours

Choose one:

- ECON 202 — Economic Statistics I
- STAT 200 — Statistical Analysis
- STAT 212 — Biostatistics
- CS 361 — Probability & Statistics for Computer Science

## Major Electives

Complete **four 400-level Economics courses (12 hours)** selected from an approved list of computation-focused courses. Required economics coursework, including these electives, must total at least **24 hours**. The catalog does not enumerate the approved list.

Source discrepancy: the [department's CS + ECON requirements page](https://economics.illinois.edu/academics/undergraduate-program/majors-minor/cs-econ/requirements) says any 400-level ECON course is approved and explicitly acknowledges the catalog's narrower wording. The 2026–2027 catalog's approved computation-focused list requirement is retained above; the catalog does not resolve this difference.
